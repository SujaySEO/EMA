## Findings

1. The year-over-year decline is mostly a loss of branded and high-intent queries. "keyword escort" (63 clicks) and "escort marketing" (32 to 10, position 1.9 to 24.8) account for much of the click drop, and the homepage position fell from 27 to 58.
2. Impressions are healthier than clicks (-34% vs -65%). Average position slipped as the site added many more pages at positions 40 to 70.
3. Recent weeks are recovering: clicks are up 4x on the last 28 days, but this is a small base (122 clicks).
4. Quick wins: /erotic-massage-seo/ (positions 7 to 8, zero clicks on "adult massage parlours seo", 179 impressions), /adult-seo-services-australia/ and /erotic-massage-seo-australia/ (page two, 400 to 800 impressions, zero clicks).
5. Cannibalization: "adult seo services" and "strippers seo" are spread across 3 to 6 pages each.
